## Method
1. Prefer primary legal texts (acts, rules, schedules) over secondary summaries.
2. Record the exact section/schedule where obtainable; "UNCERTAIN" where not.
3. Have a Nepal-based reviewer check every row before submission.
4. Publish the whole table with a datasheet: coverage, gaps, uncertainty, review date.
