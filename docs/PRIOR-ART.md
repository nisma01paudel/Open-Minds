# Prior art we build on (and credit publicly)

The competition pitch names these before a judge can. Detection is solved; we do not claim it.

| Work | What it gives us |
|---|---|
| Kincey et al. 2023 — national 30 m Nepal rainfall-triggered susceptibility, CC-BY, Zenodo 8307964, served in the government BIPAD portal | prior-risk input; we do not recompute susceptibility |
| LhamNepal / Pradhan & Kim 2025 — national hazard + IMERG rainfall thresholds, deployed in Google Earth Engine | prior art, named openly |
| Practical Action → DHM (May 2025) — rainfall intensity–duration thresholds integrated into DHM operational forecasting | the warning that already exists; we are not competing with it |
| Sen12Landslides, Scientific Data 2025 — 75k annotations, Sentinel-1 + Sentinel-2 + DEM, event dates | detection dataset/tooling |
| HR-GLDD, ESSD 2023 — includes **Rasuwa, Nepal** | a Nepal tile set already exists; we do not claim the first Nepali benchmark |
| TerraTrack, NHESS 2026 (MIT) — open optical change tracking for slow-moving landslides | change detection is a commodity; we call or reproduce it |
| Alruqimi et al. 2026, Remote Sensing 18(11):1821 — open-weight Mistral-7B writes priority-ranked landslide reports | the closest work; our rubric source (expert rating 58/30/8/4 Very Good/Good/Acceptable/Poor) |
| MAS-LAND 2026, IJDRR — multi-agent landslide detection + rapid response | we must exceed it on dispatch, not detection |
| Leder et al. EGU24-10697 / SSRN 5399352 — radar + optical monitoring of a Nepali slow-moving landslide during monsoon | the sensing is published; we do not claim to have solved the monsoon |
| Prakop Alert (ICIMOD) — free Nepali + English hazard app | the localisation lane has a credible occupant for weather, not per-slope advisories |

## The gap
Every work above ends at a **finding** — a mask, a map, a report, a probability. None ends at a **named
institution with a mandate citation, in Nepali, with the evidence age stated and a refusal to guess when
blind.** That translation is what Pahiro builds.
