Pahiro Watch

Every one of the 613 documented landslide-prone slopes in the benchmark, evaluated on a single day from one rainfall read covering the whole country — with the office legally responsible for each. This is not detection. It is the national picture of which slopes are loaded, and who is on the hook for them.
